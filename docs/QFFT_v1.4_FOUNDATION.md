## 1. The Fundamental Substance

The universe is composed of a single substance: dipole magnetic flux.

This is not a field defined over spacetime. It is not a medium through which particles move. It is the substance from which all phenomena - particles, forces, radiation, gravity, dark matter - emerge as different states and configurations of the same underlying thing.

The prior formulation of QFFT described particles as topological knots in a flux field, implying a distinction between medium and matter. That distinction is now withdrawn. There is no medium separate from matter. There is only dipole magnetic flux in various states of densification and topological configuration.

The shift is precise and consequential:

| Prior Formulation (v1.3) | Revised Formulation (v1.4) |
|--------------------------|---------------------------|
| Flux field is the medium | Flux is the substance |
| Particles are knots in the field | Particles are flux that has undergone state change |
| Medium and matter are distinct | Medium and matter are the same substance |
| Boundary between knot and field | No boundary - only a density gradient |
| Mass as temporal folding of flux | Mass as local flux density |

## 2. The Three States of Flux

Dipole magnetic flux exists in three distinct states, directly analogous to the states of water:

### 2.1 Free Flux - The Ambient State

Undensified, topologically unbound flux propagating through space. This is the dominant state of the universe by volume. Free flux carries energy in wave transfer form - oscillating dipole patterns propagating at speed `c`. What we call light, gravitational waves, and all field radiation are wave phenomena in free flux. There are no separate electromagnetic or gravitational fields. There is only free flux, oscillating.

Analogy: liquid water - fluid, wave-propagating, the ambient medium.

### 2.2 Dense Flux - The Dark State

Flux that has undergone local densification without topological state change. Gravitationally active due to its increased density, but electromagnetically transparent because it carries no topological twist - no charge, no spin, no magnetic moment. It neither emits nor absorbs wave energy resonantly.

This is dark matter. Not a new particle. Not a new substance. Simply flux that has densified past a threshold without crossing into topological knotting. The density is real and gravitationally significant. The absence of topology is why it is electromagnetically invisible.

Analogy: supercooled water - denser than ambient, not yet crystallized, no lattice structure.

### 2.3 Knotted Flux - The Particle State

Flux that has undergone both densification and topological twisting into a stable knotted configuration. This is what we call matter. The knot is not a foreign object inserted into the flux - it is flux, in a different state. The substance is continuous across the state boundary. Only the topology changes.

Knotted flux carries:

- Mass - from local flux density exceeding the knotting threshold
- Charge - from the direction of topological twist
- Spin - from the intrinsic rotation geometry of the twist
- Magnetic moment - from the dipole structure of the knotted configuration
- Higher resonant frequencies - directly from increased density, as a structural consequence of the state change

Analogy: ice - same water molecules, crystalline topology, higher resonant frequency than liquid, floats within the liquid it emerged from.

## 3. The State Change Mechanism - Topological Twisting

### 3.1 The Trigger

The transition from free flux to knotted flux requires two simultaneous conditions:

1. Sufficient local density - flux must be compressed past a threshold value
2. Rotational instability - the compressed flux must undergo topological twisting

Neither condition alone produces a stable particle. Density without twisting produces dense flux (dark matter). Twisting without sufficient density produces a transient vortex that dissipates. Both together produce a stable topological knot - a particle.

### 3.2 Matter and Antimatter - The Boat Wake Mechanism

The topological twisting mechanism has a precise and unavoidable consequence: it always produces counter-rotating pairs.

When sufficient local pressure causes free flux to undergo topological densification, the twist propagates in two directions simultaneously - clockwise and counter-clockwise - exactly as rotating eddies form on either side of a boat moving through water.

```text
        pressure event
             v
    <<<<<<  ||  >>>>>>
   (counter- |  (clockwise
   clockwise)|  twist)
      v      |      v
  antimatter |   matter
  particle   |   particle
  chi = -1   |   chi = +1
```

The boat wake analogy is mechanistically exact, not merely illustrative:

- The two eddies are mirror images - opposite rotation, same substance
- They form simultaneously and in exactly equal numbers
- Neither is more real or more primary than the other
- The trigger event (the boat, the pressure threshold) does not prefer one rotation
- They persist stably once formed, until an annihilation event returns them to free flux

This is why Sakharov's three baryogenesis conditions are unnecessary. The apparent matter-antimatter asymmetry in our observable universe is not evidence of unequal production - it is evidence of domain separation. Matter and antimatter were produced in equal quantities, then separated into distinct cosmic domains by the large-scale rotational structure of the universe, precisely as boat eddies separate to opposite sides of the wake.

Global baryon number: zero. Local baryon number: non-zero by domain. The asymmetry is observational, not ontological.

### 3.3 Thermodynamic and Pressure Dependence

Like all phase transitions, flux state changes are thermodynamically and pressure-dependent. The conditions governing knotting stability are analogous to the pressure-temperature phase diagram of water:

- High energy density environments (early universe, stellar interiors, black hole boundaries) favor knotting - sufficient density and rotational energy available
- Low energy density environments (intergalactic voids) favor free flux - insufficient density to maintain knot topology
- Intermediate environments produce metastable configurations - the particle zoo of the Standard Model reflects different stable topological configurations available at different energy thresholds

Particle mass hierarchy is therefore a topological phase diagram. Heavier particles are higher-density knot configurations requiring more extreme conditions to form and sustain. They decay to lower-density configurations (lighter particles) when environmental pressure drops below their stability threshold - releasing the density difference as wave energy into free flux.

## 4. Wave Energy Transfer

### 4.1 The Mechanism

Free flux carries energy as propagating dipole wave patterns. This is the sole mechanism of energy transfer in the universe. There are no force-carrying particles (virtual photons, gravitons, gluons as separate entities). There is only wave propagation through the free flux medium.

The relationship between knotted flux (particles) and wave energy (radiation) is resonant coupling:

- Emission: a knot transitions to a lower-density topological state, releasing the energy difference as an oscillating wave pattern into surrounding free flux. The wave frequency is determined by the energy gap between topological states - this is the origin of $E = hf$.
- Absorption: an incoming wave pattern in free flux couples resonantly to a knot whose natural frequency matches the wave frequency. The knot absorbs the wave energy and transitions to a higher-density topological state.
- Propagation: between emission and absorption, energy travels as a self-sustaining oscillation in free flux at speed `c` - a toroidal vortex structure, not a particle.

### 4.2 Why $E = hf$ Is Not a Postulate

In standard quantum mechanics, $E = hf$ is an empirical relationship elevated to a postulate. In the dipole flux ontology, it is a derived consequence:

Planck's constant $h$ is not a property of light. It is a property of knotted flux - specifically, the quantization scale of angular momentum in topological knot configurations. Because knots can only transition between discrete topological states, the energy they emit or absorb comes in discrete packets. Because wave frequency is determined by the energy of the transition, and transitions are quantized, frequencies are quantized. $E = hf$ follows necessarily.

### 4.3 The Ice Resonance Principle

Knotted flux (particles) resonates at higher frequencies than free flux for the same reason ice resonates at higher frequencies than liquid water: density increases the natural resonant frequency of any oscillating system.

This has direct physical consequences:

- The characteristic frequencies of particles (rest mass energy $mc^2$) are determined by their knot density
- Interactions between particles and waves are frequency-selective - only waves whose frequency matches the knot's resonant structure couple efficiently
- This is the physical basis of quantum selectivity: not a mysterious probabilistic rule, but straightforward resonance physics between different density states of the same substance

## 5. Reformulation of Key Physical Concepts

Under the dipole flux ontology, the standard physical concepts are reformulated without loss of predictive power but with significant gain in mechanistic clarity:

### Mass

Old: a fundamental property of particles, origin unknown  
New: local flux density of a knotted configuration - more densified flux = more mass. The Higgs mechanism describes the conditions under which flux achieves sufficient density to maintain stable knotting, not a separate field imparting mass to otherwise massless particles.

### Charge

Old: a fundamental property, positive or negative by definition  
New: the direction of topological twist - clockwise vs. counter-clockwise. Opposite charges attract because counter-rotating twists in free flux create a gradient that draws the knots together. Like charges repel because co-rotating twists create a gradient that pushes them apart.

### Spin

Old: intrinsic angular momentum, no classical analogue  
New: the intrinsic rotation geometry of the flux knot. Half-integer spin arises naturally from the topology of flux knots that require 720-degree rotation to return to their original configuration - a known property of certain topological structures.

### Gravity

Old: curvature of spacetime caused by mass-energy  
New: flux density gradient. All knotted flux (mass) creates a local increase in flux density that falls off with distance. Free flux and other knots follow the path of least resistance - the steepest density gradient. What GR calls geodesics in curved spacetime are flux density gradient descent paths in flat flux space. The predictions are identical in tested regimes. The ontology is fundamentally different.

### Light (Electromagnetic Radiation)

Old: quantized photon particles with wave-particle duality  
New: toroidal vortex waves propagating through free flux, created by the rotation of a knot's magnetic moment axis. No photon particle exists between emission and absorption. The wave is real and continuous. The apparent particle behavior at detection arises from quantized energy transfer to absorbing knots - discreteness in the knot, not in the wave.

### Dark Matter

Old: unknown particle species, electromagnetically invisible  
New: dense flux below the topological knotting threshold. No new particle. No new substance. Flux that densified without twisting - gravitationally active, electromagnetically invisible by necessity.

### Dark Energy

Old: cosmological constant, unknown origin  
New: the baseline energy density of free flux across cosmic scales - the ambient wave energy of the medium itself creating an effective pressure that drives expansion.

## 6. The Singularity Resolution

Under the dipole flux ontology, the Penrose-Hawking singularity theorems remain mathematically valid but physically irrelevant. They prove geodesic incompleteness in a spacetime curvature model. But spacetime curvature is an approximation of flux density gradients - an approximation that breaks down precisely at the extreme densities where singularities are predicted.

At sufficient density, the flux phase diagram takes over. The physics does not permit infinite density - it permits phase transition. At black hole cores, flux reaches the maximum stable knotting density, analogous to the maximum packing density of ice crystals. The singularity is the model failing, not the universe failing.

The three independent singularity resolutions from QFFT v1.3 (shell theorem, Pauli exclusion, topological information broadcasting) all remain valid and are now understood as three expressions of the same underlying constraint: flux cannot compress past its own phase transition boundary.
